Online Ruqyah Based on the Qur'an and Sunnah

Learn how to arrange an online Qur'an and Sunnah based Ruqyah session from your own location.

Online Ruqyah From Anywhere

Online sessions can provide a convenient way for people in different countries to connect remotely. The session focuses on Qur'anic recitation, permissible supplications and seeking Allah's help.

01

Remote Access

Connect from your home or another suitable private location.

02

Flexible Location

Suitable for clients who live in different cities or countries.

03

Clear Process

Appointment details and session instructions are provided clearly.

How Does Online Ruqyah Work?

The process is simple. First, submit an appointment request with your preferred date and time. After the appointment is arranged, you will receive the relevant instructions for connecting to the session.

1

Submit Your Request

Complete the appointment form with your basic information and preferred session time.

2

Confirm the Session

Your appointment details can be discussed and confirmed through the available contact method.

3

Prepare Your Space

Choose a quiet and private place where you can remain attentive during the session.

4

Attend the Session

Connect at the agreed time and participate in the session.

Before Your Online Ruqyah Session

Preparing a suitable environment can make it easier to participate calmly and attentively.

What You Can Do

  • Choose a quiet and private place.
  • Make sure your internet connection is reasonably stable.
  • Keep your phone or computer charged.
  • Join at the agreed appointment time.
  • Keep the environment comfortable and free from unnecessary distractions.

What You May Need

  • A smartphone, tablet or computer.
  • Internet access.
  • Earphones or headphones if preferred.
  • A private space for the session.
  • Your appointment information.

During the Session

A Calm and Respectful Session

The session may include Qur'anic recitation and permissible supplications. You may be asked to listen attentively and participate where appropriate.

The exact session structure can depend on the service selected and the circumstances discussed before the appointment.

Ruqyah Noor does not claim that an online session guarantees a particular result. The outcome is ultimately with Allah.

Privacy and Respect

Your personal concerns should be treated with respect.

Please only share information that is relevant to arranging or conducting your session. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information through ordinary online messages.

Ruqyah and Medical Care

Online Ruqyah should not be understood as a replacement for necessary medical or mental-health care. Physical or emotional symptoms can have different causes and may require assessment by an appropriate healthcare professional.

Important

If you are experiencing a medical emergency or serious symptoms, seek appropriate emergency or professional medical assistance rather than delaying care for an online Ruqyah session.

Ready to Arrange an Online Session?

Submit your preferred date and time through the appointment page. You can then discuss the session details through the available contact method.
