ABOUT RUQYAH

What is Ruqyah?

Ruqyah Shariah refers to permissible Quranic recitation, supplications and prayers seeking protection, comfort and healing from Allah.

The Quran contains verses that Muslims recite for protection, remembrance and seeking help from Allah.
