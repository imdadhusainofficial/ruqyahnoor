Questions About Ruqyah?

If you are considering Ruqyah for yourself or someone in your family, these frequently asked questions may help you understand what Ruqyah is, how our sessions work and what you can expect before making an appointment.

General Questions About Ruqyah

What is Ruqyah?

Ruqyah is the recitation of Qur'anic verses, authentic supplications and permissible remembrance seeking benefit and protection from Allah.

Islamic Ruqyah should be free from shirk, prohibited practices and unknown formulas.

Is Ruqyah based on the Qur'an and Sunnah?

Yes. At Ruqyah Noor, our approach is based on Qur'anic recitation, authentic supplications and principles consistent with the Qur'an and Sunnah.

For more information, you can read our About Ruqyah page.

Can I perform Ruqyah on myself?

Yes. A person can perform permissible self-Ruqyah by reciting Qur'anic verses, authentic supplications and asking Allah for protection and well-being.

Our approach also encourages people to learn appropriate self-Ruqyah rather than becoming unnecessarily dependent on another person.

Does Ruqyah guarantee a specific result?

No. We do not promise or guarantee a particular spiritual or health outcome.

Ruqyah is an act of supplication and worship. Any benefit is ultimately from Allah, and individual experiences can differ.

Online Ruqyah Questions

What is an online Ruqyah session?

An online Ruqyah session allows you to attend a Ruqyah session remotely without travelling to a physical location.

You can learn more about the process on our Online Ruqyah page.

How does an online Ruqyah session work?

The process begins with an appointment request. After the session details are confirmed, you prepare a quiet and suitable space and attend the session remotely.

The exact arrangements can be discussed when your appointment is confirmed.

Can I attend an online Ruqyah session from another country?

Online sessions can make Ruqyah guidance accessible to people who are not in the same location.

When requesting an appointment, provide your country and preferred contact details so that suitable arrangements can be discussed.

What should I prepare before an online Ruqyah session?

Choose a quiet and private place where you can participate without unnecessary interruptions.

Keep your phone or computer charged and make sure your internet connection is suitable for the session.

What happens during the online session?

The session focuses on Qur'anic recitation, permissible supplications and appropriate guidance related to Ruqyah.

We do not claim to diagnose hidden spiritual causes from symptoms, and we do not promise a particular outcome.

Appointment Questions

How do I book a Ruqyah appointment?

You can use our online appointment form to submit your basic information and preferred date and time.

Start your request from the Appointment page.

What information do I need to provide when booking?

The appointment form may ask for basic information such as your name, country, city, age, gender, preferred language, WhatsApp contact and preferred appointment time.

Please provide only information that is relevant to arranging your appointment.

Can I request a preferred date and time?

Yes. You can submit your preferred date and time through the appointment form.

The requested time should be treated as a preference until the appointment is confirmed.

What if I need to ask a question before booking?

You can visit our Contact page to get in touch before submitting an appointment request.

Ruqyah and Medical Care

Can Ruqyah replace medical treatment?

Ruqyah should not be presented as a replacement for necessary medical care.

If you have physical or psychological symptoms, seek appropriate advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Ruqyah and seeking appropriate medical care do not need to be treated as mutually exclusive.

Can you diagnose whether someone has jinn, sihr or the evil eye?

We do not claim to diagnose a specific hidden spiritual cause based only on symptoms or personal experiences.

Our focus is on permissible Qur'anic recitation, authentic supplications and responsible Ruqyah guidance.

Should I stop taking prescribed medicine because of Ruqyah?

No. Do not stop prescribed medication or necessary medical treatment without discussing it with the healthcare professional responsible for your care.

Family and Children

Can a child participate in a Ruqyah session?

Parents or guardians should arrange and supervise participation for children as appropriate to the child's age and circumstances.

When requesting an appointment for a child, provide the relevant information so that the arrangements can be discussed beforehand.

Can a family member be present during an online session?

Where appropriate, a family member or guardian may be present, particularly when support or supervision is needed.

Any specific arrangements can be discussed when the appointment is confirmed.

An important note:
Ruqyah Noor does not offer guaranteed healing, fortune-telling, divination, shirk-based practices, unknown formulas or claims of certain knowledge about hidden matters. Our approach focuses on permissible Qur'anic recitation, authentic supplications and responsible guidance.
