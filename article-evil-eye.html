Ruqyah for the Evil Eye

Learn what Islam teaches about the evil eye and how to seek Allah's protection through authentic supplications and Qur'anic recitation.

What Is the Evil Eye in Islam?

The evil eye, commonly called Al-'Ayn in Arabic, is acknowledged in authentic Islamic teachings. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said that the evil eye is real.

Muslims seek Allah's protection through supplication, remembrance and permissible Ruqyah. However, ordinary illness, tiredness, family difficulties or emotional distress should not automatically be attributed to the evil eye.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 5740; Sahih Muslim 2187.

Important: This article provides general Islamic educational information. It cannot determine whether a particular person has been affected by the evil eye, magic or any other unseen cause.

How Can Muslims Seek Protection?

A Muslim can seek Allah's protection through established acts of worship and supplication, including:

  • Reciting Surah Al-Ikhlas, Surah Al-Falaq and Surah An-Nas.
  • Maintaining morning and evening remembrance.
  • Making authentic supplications for protection and well-being.
  • Praying regularly and placing trust in Allah.
  • Avoiding superstitions, charms and practices that conflict with Islamic teachings.

1. Recite Surah Al-Falaq

Surah Al-Falaq teaches believers to seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak from various forms of evil, including the evil of an envier when they envy.

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ

Meaning: Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak."

Reference: Qur'an 113:1. Read the complete Surah Al-Falaq from a reliable Qur'an text.

2. Recite Surah An-Nas

Surah An-Nas teaches believers to seek refuge in Allah, the Lord and King of mankind, from the whispering of evil.

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ

Meaning: Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind."

Reference: Qur'an 114:1. Read the complete Surah An-Nas from a reliable Qur'an text.

3. A Supplication for Protection

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

A'udhu bi kalimatillahit-tammati min sharri ma khalaq.

Meaning: I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.

This is an authentic supplication for seeking Allah's refuge from harm.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2708.

4. A Supplication Used in Ruqyah

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ، مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ يُؤْذِيكَ، مِنْ شَرِّ كُلِّ نَفْسٍ أَوْ عَيْنٍ حَاسِدٍ، اللَّهُ يَشْفِيكَ، بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ

Bismillahi arqika, min kulli shay'in yu'dhika, min sharri kulli nafsin aw 'aynin hasid, Allahu yashfika, bismillahi arqika.

Meaning: In the name of Allah, I perform Ruqyah for you, from everything that harms you, from the evil of every soul or envious eye. May Allah heal you. In the name of Allah, I perform Ruqyah for you.

This supplication is reported in a narration describing the Prophet ﷺ performing Ruqyah.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2186.

How to Perform Permissible Ruqyah

  1. Begin with sincere faith in Allah and ask Him for help.
  2. Recite Qur'anic passages, including Al-Fatihah and the three protective chapters.
  3. Make authentic supplications for protection and healing.
  4. Follow established Sunnah practices without inventing special rituals or guaranteed results.
  5. Continue your prayers, remembrance and everyday responsibilities.

What Should You Avoid?

  • Do not accuse another person of causing the evil eye without evidence.
  • Do not assume every illness or setback has a supernatural explanation.
  • Avoid charms, talismans, unknown invocations and practices involving shirk.
  • Do not trust anyone who claims certain knowledge of the unseen or guarantees a cure.
  • Do not stop prescribed medication or delay necessary healthcare.

When Should You Seek Additional Help?

If you experience persistent physical symptoms, anxiety, sleep difficulties or other concerning changes, consult a qualified healthcare professional. Spiritual practices and appropriate healthcare can be pursued together.

If you want religious guidance, choose a trustworthy, knowledgeable person who follows the Qur'an and Sunnah and respects your safety, dignity and privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the evil eye mentioned in authentic Islamic sources?

Yes. Authentic hadith reports affirm the reality of the evil eye. Muslims are taught to seek Allah's protection through permissible means.

Can I perform Ruqyah on myself?

Yes. You can recite Qur'anic passages and authentic supplications for yourself and ask Allah for protection and well-being.

Does every difficulty mean that I have the evil eye?

No. Difficulties can have many causes. Avoid drawing conclusions about unseen causes from symptoms alone, and seek appropriate help when needed.

Conclusion

Islam teaches believers to seek Allah's protection through the Qur'an, authentic supplications and remembrance. Practise permissible Ruqyah with sincerity, avoid superstition and accusations, and take appropriate care of your physical and mental well-being.
