Our Ruqyah Shariah Services

Ruqyah Noor provides services based on Qur'anic recitation, permissible supplications and remembrance of Allah. Ruqyah is a means of seeking Allah's help; no particular outcome or guaranteed healing is promised.

Our Approach to Ruqyah Shariah

Ruqyah Noor aims to keep its approach within Islamic principles and does not use practices that are contrary to the Qur'an and Sunnah.

  • No claims of guaranteed healing
  • No fortune-telling or divination
  • No practices involving shirk
  • No unknown or unexplained formulas
  • No promises of specific spiritual outcomes
  • No instruction to abandon necessary medical care
